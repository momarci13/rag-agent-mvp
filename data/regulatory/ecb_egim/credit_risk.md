# ECB guide to internal models -- Credit risk (representative structure)

> **PLACEHOLDER / NOT VERBATIM.** Hand-authored summary of the *kind* of
> content publicly associated with the credit-risk chapter of the ECB guide
> to internal models. Not a reproduction of the ECB text; replace before
> reliance.

## Areas commonly covered

1. **Data maintenance** for the IRB approach -- the reference data set, its
   representativeness and history, and the human-judgement framework.
2. **PD** -- rating philosophy, calibration to the long-run average default
   rate, one-year default rate calculation, and calibration back-testing.
3. **LGD / ELBE** -- realised loss and recovery computation, discounting,
   direct/indirect costs, incomplete workouts, and downturn adjustment
   (cross-reference EBA/GL/2019/03).
4. **Treatment of defaulted assets** -- ELBE and LGD in-default.
5. **CCF / EAD** -- realised conversion factors, treatment of limit changes.
6. **Margin of Conservatism** -- consistent with EBA/GL/2017/16, categories
   A/B/C, quantification and release conditions.
7. **Model-related deficiencies and findings** -- severity, remediation
   ownership and deadlines, and follow-up in the next validation cycle.

## Why this matters for validation

Provides the retrieval context for the credit-risk validation agent's
qualitative findings and complements the deterministic PD-calibration,
LGD back-testing, MoC, data-quality and representativeness gates.
