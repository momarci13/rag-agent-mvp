## Overarching principles

Model risk is managed institution-wide; the internal model framework is
proportionate to the materiality of the models it covers.

## Governance and internal validation

- The **internal validation function is independent** of model development
  and reports in a way that preserves that independence.
- Validation is performed at least annually for material models, and on a
  defined cadence otherwise, with escalation triggers between full cycles.
- Roles of the management body, model owners, internal audit (third line)
  and the validation function (second line) are defined.

## Data quality

- Accuracy, completeness and appropriateness of data used for development,
  calibration and application; a data-quality framework consistent with
  BCBS 239; a register of data deficiencies feeding the Margin of
  Conservatism.

## Use test and model tiering

- Evidence that models are genuinely used in decision-making.
- Models tiered by materiality (e.g. Tier 1 high / Tier 2 medium / Tier 3
  low) with revalidation frequency and sign-off requirements scaled to tier.

## Roll-out and permanent partial use (PPU)

- A credible roll-out plan for the IRB approach; PPU limited to permitted
  exposure classes and monitored so it does not become a cherry-picking
  device.

## Model changes and third-party involvement

- Changes/extensions classified per Delegated Regulation (EU) 529/2014
  (`../model_change_529_2014/`); ex-ante approval or notification as
  required.
- Where vendors or group models are used, the institution retains
  understanding, validation responsibility and documentation.

## Why this matters for validation

The deterministic gate raises findings when the case file does not attest
validation-function independence, or when the last validation is past the
tier-appropriate revalidation cadence.
